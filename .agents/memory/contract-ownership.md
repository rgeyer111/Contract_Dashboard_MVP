---
name: Contract ownership
description: Product decision for the initial owner of a newly uploaded contract.
---

New contracts default to the person who uploads them. The owner can be reassigned during later review if needed.

**Why:** The user selected the uploader as the practical MVP default, keeping every upload accountable without requiring an assignment step.

**How to apply:** Pre-fill the owner in upload and extraction-review flows, and retain a visible owner in list and detail views.
